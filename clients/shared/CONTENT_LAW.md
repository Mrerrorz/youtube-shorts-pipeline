# CONTENT_LAW.md

## Purpose
This file defines mandatory content quality rules for Silent Capital.
All generated content must feel sharp, psychologically insightful, and premium.

---

## Always Required

- one clear insight
- one psychological mechanism
- one practical takeaway
- one memorable line
- one concrete example when possible

---

## Content Style

Tone must be:
- sharp
- calm
- observational
- intelligent

Avoid:
- motivational tone
- preachy language
- exaggerated claims

---

## Script Structure

Every short must follow:

Hook → Insight → Mechanism → Takeaway

---

## Hook Rules

Hook must:
- create curiosity immediately
- create tension in first line
- avoid introduction phrases

Forbidden:
- "Today we will discuss"
- "Did you know"
- "Here are 3 reasons"

---

## Forbidden Script Types

Reject content if:
- generic finance advice
- vague success philosophy
- internet cliché wording
- weak abstract statements

---

## Strong Script Standard

A script must teach one sharp thing viewers remember later.

---

## Visual Rules

Visuals must feel realistic.

Allowed:
- shopping
- money handling
- banking
- business environments
- realistic product visuals

Avoid:
- fantasy AI scenes
- random unrelated stock footage

---

## Caption Rules

Captions must be:
- short
- punchy
- phrase-based

Never full sentence subtitles as emphasis captions.

---

## Final Test

Ask before approval:

Would a viewer pause because this feels unexpectedly true?

If no, reject.
