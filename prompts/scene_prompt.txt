Rules:
- Produce 5 to 8 scenes. Total duration must be 60-80 seconds.
- Each scene is 6-15 seconds. Hook gets the longest dwell (8-12s) so the visual lands.
- Each scene maps to ONE clear visual the viewer holds in mind.
- visual_query: a 3-6 word phrase a stock-footage search can match. Realistic, business-documentary feel. Examples: "phone scrolling shopping app", "hand counting cash table", "credit card checkout swipe". Avoid abstract metaphors.
- caption: 2-5 word punchline phrase that will appear on screen. Punchy, not a full sentence.
- emotion: one of curiosity | tension | recognition | discomfort | resolution.

Forbidden visuals:
- text-only screens
- generic motivational imagery (sunsets, rocket ships, gold coins falling)
- fantasy or surreal scenes
- random unrelated footage

Output STRICT JSON only:

  "scenes": [
    {{"scene": 1, "duration": 10, "visual_query": "...", "caption": "...", "emotion": "curiosity"}},

  ]
